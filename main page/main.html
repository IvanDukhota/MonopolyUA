Rules

The goal of the game

Become the richest player by buying up streets and charging rent to your rivals.

Initial capital

Each player receives the same amount of money and a chip, which he moves around the field.

Actions on cells

When you stop on the street: you can buy it if it is not occupied. If it is occupied, you pay rent.

Taxes and fines

On special fields you will have to pay taxes or fines, which go to the bank.

Chance and Community Treasury cards

Draw cards and perform the indicated actions - they can bring both bonuses and losses.

Win

The one who stays afloat and ruins his rivals wins.
